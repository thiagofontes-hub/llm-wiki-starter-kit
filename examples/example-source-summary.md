## Summary

The landing page/abstract for a well-known conference talk (delivered at Lead Developer and Write/Speak/Code) about "glue work" — the essential, coordination-heavy work that keeps a technical team functioning but rarely shows up in a job title or promotion packet. The extracted text is limited to the talk's abstract; the slides, video, and sketchnotes referenced on the page were not retrievable due to a cross-origin iframe restriction. Its contribution is a concrete, named vocabulary term for a pattern the wiki already discusses more abstractly.

## Key Contributions

- **Glue Work** — the named category of coordination/support tasks (onboarding, roadmap, user contact, gap-noticing, design-doc questions) that hold a team together without being anyone's formal deliverable
- **Deliberate Glue Work Allocation** — glue work builds leadership skill when consciously distributed and valued, but becomes career-limiting when absorbed silently by default
